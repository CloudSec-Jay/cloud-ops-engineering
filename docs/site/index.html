How I work

Understand the system.
Make the change count.

My approach to operating infrastructure starts with a clear problem and a plan for what happens next.

Start a conversation →
  1. 01 / Understand

    Find the failure boundary.

    Trace dependencies, inspect logs, and establish what changed before choosing a fix.

  2. 02 / Change

    Keep the change controlled.

    Scope access, automate repeatable steps, and define rollback before applying a change.

  3. 03 / Follow through

    Check the outcome.

    Confirm service health, document the result, and leave a runbook that helps the next operator.
